Terms & privacy

This is an independent, client-side utility. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Banner, Ellucian, or any university.

Your selected PDF is processed locally in your browser. The application does not upload, store, or transmit the PDF to an application server. Do not upload documents containing information you do not want displayed on your screen.

The timetable is provided for convenience. Check the original university schedule for official registration, room, time, and academic information. Use of this tool is at your own discretion.

Created by Samir Daadouch. For bug reports or technical issues, email b00110561@aus.edu.
